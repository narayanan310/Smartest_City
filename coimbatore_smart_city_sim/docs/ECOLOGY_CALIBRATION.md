# Ecology calibration and sensitivity

Ambient temperature 32.5 deg C, initial dissolved oxygen 5.50 mg/L, 30 seeds, shipped `DO_SHADE_EFFECTIVENESS` = 2.1.

Generated by `scripts/fit_shading.py`. Analysis only: no model default is changed by this script.

## 1. Which coefficient actually drives the result

Each coefficient is swept across its plausible range with everything else held fixed, at 15% coverage. The span is the worst-case movement in minimum dissolved oxygen attributable to that one number.

| Coefficient | min DO at low | min DO at high | span (mg/L) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `gross_photosynthesis_mg_l_per_hr` | 2.990 | 6.070 | 3.080 |
| `shade_effectiveness` | 5.000 | 3.170 | 1.830 |
| `water_temp_base_c` | 4.810 | 3.530 | 1.280 |
| `algae_suppression_credit` | 4.170 | 5.320 | 1.150 |
| `respiration_mg_l_per_hr` | 2.980 | 2.000 | 0.980 |
| `shading_hours` | 5.210 | 4.360 | 0.850 |
| `reaeration_k_per_hr` | 6.530 | 7.310 | 0.780 |
| `aeration_rate_mg_l_per_hr` | 4.330 | 4.330 | 0.000 |

**`gross_photosynthesis_mg_l_per_hr` is the dominant coefficient**, moving the headline by 3.08 mg/L on its own. `shade_effectiveness` is ranked 2 with a span of 1.83 mg/L.

Ranking the coefficients this way corrected an assumption in the project documentation, which had named `shade_effectiveness` as *the* uncalibrated weak point on the grounds that the shading term dominates the model. It does dominate the *sign* of the coverage response, but the gross-photosynthesis rate has the larger effect on the absolute minimum, so a calibration against a real observation should constrain both.

Inert at 15% coverage (zero span, because the aeration trigger is never reached): `aeration_rate_mg_l_per_hr`. These matter only at higher coverage.

## 2. Inverting the model against a target observation

If a dissolved-oxygen profile under the array were available, this is the calibration that would be performed: bisect each high-influence coefficient until the model reproduces the observed minimum. The two coefficients respond in opposite directions, which is why the search measures the direction instead of assuming it.

| Target min DO | coverage | coefficient | fitted value | achieved | bracketed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.00 mg/L | 15% | `gross_photosynthesis_mg_l_per_hr` | 0.6561 | 4.010 | yes |
| 4.00 mg/L | 15% | `shade_effectiveness` | 2.6376 | 4.000 | yes |
| 4.00 mg/L | 20% | `gross_photosynthesis_mg_l_per_hr` | 0.7092 | 4.010 | yes |
| 4.00 mg/L | 20% | `shade_effectiveness` | 2.0212 | 4.000 | yes |
| 5.00 mg/L | 15% | `gross_photosynthesis_mg_l_per_hr` | 0.7907 | 5.000 | yes |
| 5.00 mg/L | 15% | `shade_effectiveness` | 1.0000 | 5.000 | yes |
| 5.00 mg/L | 20% | `gross_photosynthesis_mg_l_per_hr` | 0.8549 | 5.010 | yes |
| 5.00 mg/L | 20% | `shade_effectiveness` | 1.0000 | 4.830 | NO - unattainable |

Search bounds are each coefficient's plausible range from section 1. A row reading 'unattainable' means no value of this coefficient within that range can produce the target at that coverage, which is itself a useful result: it would mean the model's structure, not its calibration, is the problem.

## 3. Sensitivity of the headline to `shade_effectiveness`

| shade effectiveness | 10% cov | 15% cov | 20% cov |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.00 | 5.170 | 5.000 | 4.830 |
| 1.50 | 4.960 | 4.690 | 4.430 |
| 2.10 | 4.720 | 4.330 | 3.940 |
| 2.50 | 4.560 | 4.080 | 3.620 |
| 3.00 | 4.350 | 3.780 | 3.210 |
| 4.00 | 3.950 | 3.170 | 2.970 |

Read across a row for the effect of lake shading strength; read down a column for the effect of array size. The ecological status boundary sits between the rows that cross 4.0 mg/L, which is why the shipped value is stated as an assumption rather than a result.

## 4. Seed robustness at the shipped value

| coverage | min DO | mean | max | span | seeds below 4.0 mg/L |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10% | 4.660 | 4.704 | 4.750 | 0.090 | 0/30 |
| 15% | 4.280 | 4.317 | 4.360 | 0.080 | 0/30 |
| 20% | 3.880 | 3.929 | 3.970 | 0.090 | 30/30 |

Seed spread comes from the stochastic algal and mixing terms. It is small relative to the sensitivity in section 3, which is the point: the dominant uncertainty is structural, not stochastic.
